# whois

## Basic Usage

whois <domain>
whois <IP>

---

## Specify WHOIS Server

whois -h <server> <target>

---

## Output Focus

Ignore noise. Extract:

- Registrant
- Organization
- Email
- Name Servers
- Net Range

---

## When to Use

- Early recon
- Domain-based targets
- Expanding attack surface

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Private registration may hide data
- Not always useful in internal lab environments
